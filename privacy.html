Privacy Policy for Still to Spend

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Still to Spend (“Fin de mois” in French) is a budgeting app for the Mac. It was designed so that your data stays yours, and only yours.

No data collected

Still to Spend doesn't collect any personal data. We never receive your expenses, your income, your categories or any information about how you use the app.

No network connection

The app never connects to the internet. In fact, it isn't allowed to: the macOS App Sandbox blocks any network access. No account to create, no bank login, no server.

Where your data is stored

All your data (expenses, categories, recurring expenses, savings goal, settings) is stored only on your Mac, in the app's private folder:

~/Library/Containers/com.hippolytecastex.findemois/

It isn't synced with iCloud or sent anywhere else. It's included in your Time Machine backups if you use them.

Export

You can export your data as CSV files from the cockpit (General > Export my data…). Exports only happen when you ask for them, to the folder you choose. What you do with those files afterwards is up to you.

Deletion

To delete all your data, delete the Still to Spend app, then the folder above if it's still there.

No tracking, no ads

Still to Spend contains no analytics, no trackers, no ads and no third-party services.
